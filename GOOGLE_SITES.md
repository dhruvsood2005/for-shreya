## Editing in Google Sites

1. Open the editor link above. In **Pages**, choose **Home** if needed.
2. Click a letter heading to edit its title. Click inside the collapsible letter to edit its body. Google Sites saves changes to the draft automatically.
3. Every letter now has a different photo immediately beneath it. Google Sites does not support images inside the hidden body of a native collapsible group, so each photo is in the directly adjacent section and remains visually paired with its letter. Select a photo to crop, resize, move, replace, or remove it. Converted JPEG copies of all 15 originals are in `google-sites/all-photos/`; the original HEIC and JPG files in Downloads were not changed.
4. The photo carousel still sits between the introduction and first letter. Select it and choose **Settings** to edit its five images, order, or captions. Those selected photos are also in the private [For Shreya Birthday Site Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_atuo6WRFlq41GefLPYngGBc798M-w5B). Because Google Sites forces every slide into one shared frame, the carousel now uses padded 4:3 JPEG copies from `google-sites/carousel-safe/`. This keeps both people fully visible on desktop and phone without changing the original photos.
5. Use **Themes** to change site-wide colors and fonts. Select a section and use **Section colors** for its background. The current warm header is a built-in Google Sites gallery image. The optional custom header artwork is at `google-sites/native-header.png`, but it has not been uploaded.
6. Use **Preview** to check desktop and phone before publishing.

The custom `dist/` site and `google-sites/for-shreya.html` are separate reference/source artifacts. Editing them does **not** change the native Google Sites page.
